# WordPress Series and Book Inventory

Snapshot date: September 19, 2026

## Scope and sources

This inventory records the series and books represented on the production WordPress site at `fickledragon.com`. It is a site-content inventory, not a complete bibliography or proof of publication status on retailer platforms.

Sources inspected through the authenticated WordPress web administration interface:

- **Pages**: 87 published pages in WordPress Admin.
- **Tools > Export > Pages**: page content, links, statuses, and custom fields from the WordPress WXR export.
- **Current public navigation**: Junkyard Pirate, Spaceship Mechanic, Oldest Starfighter, Privateer Tales, Afterwar Saga, and Witchy World.

Only published pages are counted below. Attachments and the unpublished `dp-rewrite-republish` record in the export are excluded.

## Summary

- **10 logical series** are explicitly named in the site's series modules.
- **47 book entries** appear across 7 published series landing pages.
- **6 series destinations** appear in the current primary navigation.

- Guardians of Gaeland is embedded in the Witchy World landing page.
- Tinker/Knight Adventures and Pale Ship — A Privateer Tale are embedded in the Privateer Tales landing page.

## Confirmed site discrepancies

1. The *Fool Me Once* entry on the Privateer Tales landing page links to *When Justice Calls* instead of the published *Fool Me Once* page.
2. Privateer Tales books 17 and 18 use the misspelled series label `Privater Tales`.
3. *Junkyard Saboteur* has a blank `BOOK#` field.
4. The *Privateer's Supremacy* landing-page heading contains the stale text `(MARCH 24)`.
5. The *Brigand's Choice* landing-page title and its page title disagree about the apostrophe and pluralization.
6. The Witchy World and Henry Biggston landing pages contain HTTP book links rather than HTTPS links.
7. Duplicate and placeholder pages make page counts larger than the number of distinct books.

No WordPress content was changed while producing this inventory.
